One-line: icon-only button for toolbars, close affordances and regenerate actions; never used for a page's primary action.

\`\`\`jsx
<IconButton label="Regenerate answer"><i data-lucide="refresh-cw"></i></IconButton>
\`\`\`

Props: \`label\` (required), \`bare\` for chromeless. Icons come from Lucide (see ICONOGRAPHY in readme).
